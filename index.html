Mahyar Maleki

Guest researcher at Freie Universität Berlin. Based in Berlin, Germany.

I study how the brain and artificial neural networks use time to recognise what they see.

I’m a guest researcher in Radoslaw Cichy’s Neural Dynamics of Visual Cognition lab at Freie Universität Berlin, where I compare video and image models with EEG and fMRI responses to naturalistic videos. Before that, I did an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Tehran. My thesis used EEG to compare how the brain represents videos and still images.

My interests include computational neuroscience, visual perception, temporal integration in vision, video understanding models, and explainable AI.

I’m looking for a PhD position in computational neuroscience, machine learning, or brain-inspired AI. If you think I could be a good fit for your group, please get in touch.
